Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

The Joshua Tree Moon Calendar is sold by The Purple Tornado, Inc. This policy explains what we collect and what we do with it.

When you buy a calendar

We receive your name, email address and shipping address so we can send your order. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see your full card number. Stripe handles your payment under its own privacy policy.

If an AI agent places an order for you, the agent's platform shares the details needed to complete and ship that order.

When you write to us

We keep your message and email address so we can reply.

How we use it

We use your information to ship your order, answer your questions, and handle returns. We do not sell or rent your information. We share your shipping address only with the carrier that delivers your calendar.

This website

This site does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so those services receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser loads the page.

Your information

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, write to us at lunacy@joshuatreemooncalendar.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
